# Parts list

Everything bought for one twin-drive unit, with the shops used. Prices change, so none are given.

| Part | Qty | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TEAC FD-55GFR 5.25" floppy drive | 2 | eBay | Get this exact model: the shell is sized to its frame and bezel. Sold untested is normal; one of ours arrived with a dead board. See the guide for how to check a drive before building around it. |
| Greaseweazle V4.1 | 1 | [decromancer.ca](https://decromancer.ca/greaseweazle/) | The USB floppy controller. V4 has the 34-pin connector the ribbon needs. |
| 5 V / 12 V power supply | 1 | [deviceside PSU5](https://shop.deviceside.com/prod/PSU5) | Floppy-drive Molex power. |
| Y power cable | 1 | [deviceside YCABLE](https://shop.deviceside.com/prod/YCABLE) | Splits the supply to both drives. |
| 34-pin floppy ribbon, twisted, 36" | 1 | [cablesonline 36UNFLOPDRIV](https://www.cablesonline.com/36unflopdriv.html) | Card-edge connectors for 5.25" drives. The twist is what makes the two drives A and B. |
| USB-C cable | 1 | [Amazon B06XMY49BM](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B06XMY49BM) | Board to computer. Leaves through the notch in the rear door. |
| 6-32 x 3/8" pan-head screws | 8 | [Amazon B0G6ZSQGT9](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G6ZSQGT9) | Hold the drives to the shell floor, through the washers. |
| #6 flat washers | 8 | [Amazon B0CM3MCSGS](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CM3MCSGS) | Under the drive screw heads. |
| M2.5 x 10 screws | 4 | [Amazon B0G58L9P4G](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G58L9P4G) | Hold the Greaseweazle on its posts. |
| M2.5 hex nuts | 4 | [Amazon B0FMRGFZP4](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FMRGFZP4) | Pressed into the pockets under the floor; the board screws go into them. |
| Rubber bumper feet | 4 | [Amazon B074PXFWPK](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B074PXFWPK) | Stick into the recesses underneath. |
| PETG filament | about 1.1 kg | any | Printed on a Prusa MK4S. Print settings are in the pack's README. |

Also useful, not in the box: isopropyl alcohol (90 % or stronger) and cotton swabs for cleaning the heads, and a few blank 5.25" HD disks for testing drives before any original disk goes in.
